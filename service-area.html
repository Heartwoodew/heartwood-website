Where we work.

Based in New Beith, south of Brisbane. House-and-land through the southern and western growth estates, and the surrounding south-east.

This is the area we cover. It is not a driving-time map, and it is not a promise about a particular estate.

  1. New Beith

    Home base, south of Brisbane. Logan and the Scenic Rim edge are close country from here.

  2. Brisbane south

    House sites on the south side of Brisbane.

  3. Logan

    House-and-land through Logan, including the growth estates.

  4. Ipswich

    House sites through Ipswich and the estates around it.

  5. Lowood and Somerset

    Growth estates around Lowood and through Somerset.

  6. Scenic Rim edge

    Work on the Scenic Rim edge, out from New Beith.

  7. Gold Coast northern corridor

    The northern Gold Coast corridor, as part of the surrounding south-east.

If the site is a house lot in these areas, ask. If it sits just outside them, say where it is and we will tell you straight.
