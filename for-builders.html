The job

From call-up to invoice

We work direct with the site team, day to day, and we turn jobs around fast so the program is not sitting on spoil, access or a clean.

  1. Call-up

    Ring or email. Give us the lot, the estate, the work and when the site can take the machine. We confirm we can make that part of the program.

  2. Purchase order

    Send the PO number with the call-up, or raise it in your portal. We use builder PO numbers and portals, including BusinessCraft.

  3. On site

    SWMS for the job, a prestart and a Take 5 before work starts. White Card on site. The owner holds a Certificate IV in Work Health and Safety. Public liability insurance is in place.

  4. Photos and a docket

    After every job we send photos and a docket from our own jobs system. The supervisor has a record of what was done on that lot.

  5. Invoice

    The invoice lines up with the docket and your PO number.
