Earthmoving operator / MR truck driver.

Heartwood Earthworx is a growing earthworks and site-services business working for some of SEQ's busiest home builders. We're after a reliable full-time operator who can also drive our MR/HR tipper trucks, to join our small crew based out of Chuwar.

Earthmoving Operator / MR Truck Driver, Full-time, Heartwood Earthworx (SEQ)

  • Full-time
  • $40 per hour + overtime
  • Chuwar, SEQ
  • MR licence or higher

What you'll do

  • Run skid steers and a 5t excavator on house sites: slab cleans, backblading, spoil removal, trenching and road base
  • Drive our MR/HR tipper trucks and tow our tag trailer between jobs
  • Daily prestarts, Take 5s and following SWMS on site
  • Keep sites tidy and work with builders' supervisors

What you need

  • MR licence or higher (clean driving record a must)
  • Skid steer and/or excavator experience
  • White Card
  • Reliable, punctual, good communication with supervisors
  • Tickets and towing experience a bonus

What you get

  • Full-time, steady work Monday to Friday
  • $40 per hour depending on experience, plus overtime rates
  • Well-kept machines and a phone app that lays out your day
  • A small team where you'll be valued
