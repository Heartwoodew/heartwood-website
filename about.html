A site-services crew for volume builders.

Heartwood Earthworx Pty Ltd is an earthworks and site-services subcontractor based in New Beith, Queensland. Established in 2022.

The day-to-day is with construction managers, site supervisors and procurement on lower-to-mid market house-and-land. We work direct with major volume builders.

The work is the part of the program that stalls a house when it slips: spoil off the lot, access and crossovers in, trenches, and the cleans.

Plant on the tools is a 5t excavator, skid steers and MR/HR tipper trucks. Excavator and skid steer hire is with an operator.

Safety

SWMS for the job, prestarts and Take 5s before work starts. White Card on site. The owner holds a Certificate IV in Work Health and Safety. Public liability insurance is in place.
